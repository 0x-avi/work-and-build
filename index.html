Rules

  • Apply to 0–1 year and associate roles. Mumbai and Thane first, then remote, Bangalore and Hyderabad.
  • Every cold DM leads with proof: the freight agent (100+ queries a day), the paid SaaS, or GinLo. Ask a real question, not for a job.
  • An interview outranks everything except sleep. Say yes to paid trials.
  • The weekend finish block closes the week's open items first: unfinished AI work, blog posts, PRs, Kaggle entries. Only when nothing is open do you start something new.
  • Follow up once on day 4 and once on day 9 after applying.
  • Read a full contract before accepting anything. Lock-ins, notice and exclusivity get negotiated before you start, not after.
  • Security work: only programs and targets that explicitly allow testing. Read the scope first.
  • Build in public: one post a week about what you built or learned.
